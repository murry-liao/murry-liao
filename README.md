## About me

I study low-energy heavy-ion reactions, with a focus on multinucleon transfer, heavy-ion fusion, and the production of neutron-rich nuclei and superheavy elements. My work explores shell effects, non-equilibration evolution, and reaction dynamics using transport models and coupled-channels methods.

I am also interested in stochastic dynamics, master equations, and reduced-basis emulators for nuclear reaction calculations.

## Research interests

- Multinucleon transfer and the production of neutron-rich nuclei near N = 126
- Shell effects, drift, fluctuations, and equilibration in heavy-ion collisions
- Heavy-ion fusion, coupled-channels calculations, and nuclear deformation
- Nuclear reaction emulators and Bayesian uncertainty quantification
